## 1. Ek nazar mein: kaun-kaun si file jama karni hai?

**Short answer:** Students ko **4 practical files** (Surveying, Concrete Technology, Strength of Material, Building Construction & Material) + **Summer Internship report** + **har subject ka term work/assignment file** (WRE included) jama karna hota hai. Surveying mein ek **A1 imperial drawing sheet** (traverse plot) bhi alag se jama hoti hai.

## 2. Har lab file mein kitne practical likhne hain (official list)

### 2.1 Basic Surveying (P2415301) — 12 practicals
1. Chain, tape aur ranging rod se do inter-visible stations ke beech distance measure karna.
2. Reciprocal ranging karke do stations ke beech distance measure karna.
3. Chain aur cross staff survey se open field ka area nikalna.
4. Prismatic compass se open traverse ki Fore Bearing aur Back Bearing lena.
5. 5–6 sides ke closed traverse ki FB/BB lena, local attraction correct karke included angles nikalna.
6. Survey Project — kisi building ke around minimum 5 sides ka chain & compass closed traverse.
7. Practical 6 ke data ko **A1 size imperial drawing sheet** par plot karna.
8. Dumpy level / Auto level aur levelling staff se simple levelling.
9. Differential levelling — Height of Instrument aur Rise & Fall method se RL nikalna.
10. Fly levelling with double check.
11. Contour map project — 150 m × 150 m block, 10 m × 10 m grid (block contouring).
12. Digital planimeter se irregular figure ka area measure karna.

### 2.2 Concrete Technology (P2415302) — 18 practicals
1. Cement ki fineness (90 µ sieve / Blaine's apparatus).
2. Standard consistency (Vicat apparatus).
3. Setting time — initial & final (Vicat apparatus).
4. Cement ki compressive strength (70.6 mm mortar cube).
5. Soundness (Le-Chatelier method).
6. Fine & coarse aggregate ka size, grading aur fineness modulus.
7. Flakiness index & elongation index.
8. Sand mein silt content.
9. Bulking of sand.
10. Bulk density of aggregates.
11. Water absorption of aggregates.
12. Impact value of coarse aggregate.
13. Crushing value of coarse aggregate.
14. Abrasion value (Los Angeles).
15. Workability — slump / compaction factor / flow table / Vee-Bee.
16. Concrete cube ki compressive strength (7 din & 28 din).
17. Durability — RCPT (ASTM C 1202) / Permeability test (IS 3085).
18. NDT — Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity / Rebound Hammer.

### 2.3 Strength of Material for Civil Engg. (P2415303) — 13 practicals
1. Flywheel ka moment of inertia.
2. UTM ke different components ka study.
3. Tension test on Mild steel / HYSD (IS 1608) — yield, ultimate, fracture strength, % elongation.
4. Compression test on mild steel piece (UTM).
5. Flexural test on timber beam (IS 2408), dono orientations.
6. Shear strength test.
7. Izod impact test.
8. Charpy impact test.
9. Brinell hardness test.
10. Rockwell hardness test.
11. Different types of truss ka model banana.
12. Simply supported beam ka SFD & BMD (point load + UDL).
13. Mohr's circle method se principal stresses aur principal plane.

### 2.4 Building Construction & Material (P2415304) — approx. 12 practicals
(College ke lab manual se exact list confirm karein; syllabus mein ye tests hain)
1. Stone / building material ke field tests & identification.
2. Bricks ke field tests (shape, size, colour, sound, hardness).
3. Efflorescence test on bricks.
4. Water absorption test on bricks.
5. Compressive strength of bricks / blocks.
6. Cement ki fineness (field method).
7. Cement ka initial & final setting time.
8. Cement ki compressive strength.
9. Mortar / cement-sand ratio & workability.
10. Timber ke properties / seasoning ka study.
11. Tiles, fly ash bricks, AAC blocks ka comparative study.
12. Doors, windows, stair case, roof ka labelled sketch / drawing sheet.

### 2.5 Water Resource Engineering (2415305) — koi lab practical nahi
Sirf **term work (50 marks)** jama hota hai:
- Assignment / numericals file
- Micro project (koi ek) — jaise rainwater harvesting design, sprinkler/micro-irrigation design, cropping pattern report, dam/weir site visit report
- Seminar topic + visit report (agar college ne diya ho)

### 2.6 Summer Internship – I (2415306)
- Internship **certificate** (company/office se)
- **Training report** (daily diary / weekly report + work description + photos)
- Presentation / viva

## 3. Har practical ka format (isi order mein likhein)

Har experiment ke liye ye 8 headings likhna standard hai:

1. **Practical No.**
2. **Aim / Object** — kya nikalna hai
3. **Apparatus / Equipment Required** — naam + capacity/specification
4. **Theory / Principle** — 5–8 lines + formula (e.g. Fineness Modulus, Water absorption %)
5. **Procedure** — step-wise (5–8 steps)
6. **Observation Table** — reading/weight, IS code reference
7. **Calculation & Result** — formula lagakar answer, unit ke saath
8. **Precautions** (3–5 points) + **Conclusion**

**File ke shuru mein:**
- Cover page (College name, Subject name + code, Student name, Roll/Enrollment No., Branch & Semester, Session)
- **Index page** (Practical No. | Name of Practical | Page No. | Date | Signature)
- Har page par margin, diagram pencil se, writing blue/black pen se
- Har practical ke saamne teacher ka signature + date zaroor

## 4. Marks ka breakdown (kyun file important hai)

| Component | Marks (per subject with lab) |
|-----------|------------------------------|
| Theory (PTA 30 + ETA 70) | 100 |
| Term Work (Internal 20 + External 30) | 50 |
| **Lab (PLA 20 + ELA 30)** | **50** |
| **Total** | **200** |

- Term work ka assessment **40% internal + 60% external** hota hai — matlab external examiner bhi file dekhta hai.
- **ELA (End Lab Assessment) = 30 marks**: practical perform + product/record + **viva-voce**. File complete na ho to viva aur record marks kat jaate hain.
- WRE mein total 150 marks (100 theory + 50 term work), lab nahi.

---

## 5. Submission tips

1. **Deadline:** Semester end exam se pehle college apni date deta hai — typically practical exam ke 1–2 hafte pehle. Late file par marks kat sakte hain.
2. **Apne college ka lab manual** hi final authority hai — practical ki sankhya (jaise BCM 10 ya 12) college ke hisaab se badal sakti hai.
3. **Date column** har practical mein bharein; sab ek hi date na likhein.
4. **Diagram** pencil se, scale se; graph/presentation table pen se.
5. **Survey project drawing sheet (A1)** ko roll karke jama karein, fold na karein.
6. **Internship report** par company ka stamp + certificate must hai; report A4 spiral binding karwayein.
7. Viva ke liye har practical ka **aim, formula aur ek-line result** yaad rakhein — external examiner yahi poochta hai.
